## biodiversity
BiodiversityData.R: wrangle spatial data layers from biodiversitymapping.org and IUCN Red List
BiodiversityModeling.R: wrangling/analysis of environmental characteristics of species description locations
GBIF_species_descriptions.R: investigation of using GBIF for species description locations, which may be unreliable

## climate
Extract_worldclim.R: pull worldclim climate rasters
PCA_worldclim: exploratory principal component analysis for condensing many climate rasters into fewer, composite variables

## DEM
CopernicusDEMdata.R: pull digital elevation model (DEM) data from Copernicus

## ecoregions
ecoregion_analysis.R wrangling/analysis of World Ecosystems ecoregion data, mainly the Temp_Moist classification, which corresponds to "climate zones" similar to ecoregions

## HumanFootprint
HumanFootprint.R: wrangling of Human Footprint data from Sun et al. (2026)

## HydroSheds
HydroSheds.R: wrangling of river data from HydroSheds to calculate river density

## protected_areas
ProtectedAreas.R: pull and wrangle protected area data from World Database of Protected Areas
ProtectedAreasGBIF.R: basic analysis of GBIF observations in protected areas

## roads
Roads.R: wrangling of road data from GRIP to calculate road density
